# Output licensing

The workflow engine is Apache-2.0. The original `core-topdown-v1` procedural pixel coordinates are dedicated under CC0-1.0 and no Universal LPC artwork is bundled. Deterministic UI, particles, and synthesized waveforms produced solely by the built-in CC0 recipes are recorded as CC0-1.0.

Project-supplied parts are not relicensed. Recipes and `asset-manifest.json` must record the compatible effective output license and provenance. CC-BY material retains attribution requirements; commercial, proprietary, and original material retains its declared terms. Unknown, non-commercial, no-derivatives, unverifiable, or otherwise incompatible inputs are blocked.
